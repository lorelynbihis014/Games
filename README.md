# Teacher Lhorie’s Game Hub

## Put it online (three files, no folders to drag)
1. On github.com create a new public repository, for example `game-hub`.
2. Click **Add file, then Upload files**. Drag in these three files (or click **choose your files**): `index.html`, `games.json`, `README.md`. Then click **Commit changes**.
3. Open **Settings, then Pages**. Choose **Deploy from a branch**, pick `main` and `/ (root)`, then **Save**.
4. After a minute your site is live at `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/game-hub/`. Share that link with your students.

## Add your games
1. Open your live site and go to **Dashboard**.
2. Paste your GitHub access token (the Dashboard shows how to make one). Press **Create all subject and grade folders** once.
3. Pick a subject and grade, choose your `.html` game and press **Upload game**.

Your students see new games on their own devices within about a minute.
